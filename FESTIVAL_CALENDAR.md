# Indian Urban Traffic Festival & Event Calendar (2026)

An open-source benchmark dataset of 45+ festivals, national holidays, civic elections, sporting finals, and bandhs/hartals across Indian metropolitan regions for urban traffic flow prediction and anomalous congestion analysis.

## Overview & Methodology

In Indian metropolitan traffic management, festival and abnormal event periods introduce distinct non-recurrent congestion dynamics:
1. **Pre-festival / Festival Eve Rush**: Massive outbound travel towards bus terminals, railway stations, and arterial highways 24-48 hours before the event.
2. **Shopping Corridor Saturation**: Severe congestion around commercial hubs (e.g., T. Nagar in Chennai, Chickpet in Bengaluru, Dadar in Mumbai, Chandni Chowk in Delhi) 3-5 days leading up to major retail festivals (Diwali, Pongal, Eid, Durga Puja).
3. **Event Day Pattern**: Drastic reduction in morning commute / office corridor volume combined with localized gridlock around places of worship, procession routes, and community gathering points.
4. **Post-Festival Influx**: Inbound arterial choke points as citizens return from native districts on the day following multi-day holidays.

## Regional Scope Encodings for Model Feature Tensors

When feeding calendar features into the GNN temporal embedding layer (src/models/festival_embed.py), use the 4-dimensional categorical tuple:

`python
festival_feature = [
    is_festival,           # {0: Normal day, 1: Eve, 2: Event day, 3: Post-festival}
    days_to_festival,      # Signed integer: -3, -2, -1, 0, +1, +2
    festival_type,         # {0: None, 1: Religious, 2: National, 3: Sporting, 4: Civic/Strike}
    geographic_scope,      # {0: Local, 1: Citywide, 2: State/Regional, 3: National}
]
